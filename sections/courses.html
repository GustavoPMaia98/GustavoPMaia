Courses & Formations

Training schools and specialised courses that complement my research.

Building the Hard Rocky Planets — From Mercury to the Asteroid Belt
Summer School · Institut Origines (Aix-Marseille Université) & Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris (IPGP) · Marseille, France · 22–26 June 2026

An interdisciplinary summer school co-organised by the Institut Origines at Aix-Marseille Université and the Origins theme of the Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris, dedicated to the formation and evolution of the inner Solar System, with a focus on terrestrial planets and the asteroid belt. Blending lectures by renowned experts with interactive roundtable discussions, the programme covered key topics such as cosmochemistry, planetary dynamics, disk processes, impacts, and surface composition — deepening the scientific context for my work on the exogenous delivery of organic matter and prebiotic chemistry.

Astrobiology Introductory Course (Virtual)
Course · Rencontres Exobiologiques pour Doctorants (RED) · Le Teich, France · Online · June 2026

An online edition of the RED interdisciplinary training school on the foundations of astrobiology — the study of the origin, evolution, and distribution of life in the Universe — bringing together doctoral students and young researchers from astronomy, planetology, geology, chemistry, and biology. Delivered remotely over a week of lectures and discussions (equivalent to 25 hours of training) and completed with a successful evaluation test, it broadened the scientific context for my work on prebiotic chemistry and the origin of life.

Recommendations for Publishing in International Journals
Formation · Biblioteca do Instituto Superior Técnico (BIST) · Online · February 2025

A training session organised by the Library of Instituto Superior Técnico (BIST) on best practices for publishing in international peer-reviewed journals — covering journal selection, manuscript structuring, and the submission and peer-review process. Held by videoconference on 13 February 2025.
